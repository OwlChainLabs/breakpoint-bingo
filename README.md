# 🎯 breakpoint bingo

every card is different. post yours before breakpoint, mark it live during the keynotes, tag your frens.

**get your card: [bingo.owlchainlabs.xyz](https://bingo.owlchainlabs.xyz)**

![breakpoint-bingo demo](./demo.gif)

## what it does

- gives you a random 5x5 card out of 35 breakpoint predictions
- tap a square when it happens on stage, bingo is detected automatically
- your card and marks are saved in your browser
- countdown to breakpoint
- tag friends and post your card on x, or save it as an image

## add your own predictions

all squares live in the `POOL` list in `index.html`. open a PR with your best one.

## run it yourself

one html file. no build, no keys, no backend.

```bash
git clone https://github.com/OwlChainLabs/breakpoint-bingo
open breakpoint-bingo/index.html
```

just for fun, not affiliated with solana foundation or breakpoint.
